Frequently asked questions

How do I request a tour?

Use the contact form and share your destination, preferred dates, group size and budget. The team can then respond with suitable options once the business contact details are configured.

Are the packages and prices on this website final?

No. The packages on this starter website are sample concepts. Confirm all prices, inclusions, exclusions and availability in writing before making a payment.

Can I request a customised trip?

Yes. A tailor-made itinerary can be planned around your interests, pace, travel dates and budget, subject to actual availability.

What should I pack?

Pack according to the destination, season and planned activities. Comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, personal medication and copies of important documents are common starting points.

Do I need a visa or vaccinations?

Requirements vary by nationality, route and destination. Check current official immigration and public-health guidance well before departure.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancellation and refund terms depend on the confirmed booking and supplier policies. Request these terms in writing before paying.

Travel planning checklist

  • Confirm travel dates and passport validity
  • Check visa and entry requirements
  • Review health advice and travel insurance
  • Confirm itinerary, costs and what is included
  • Keep emergency contacts and booking records
  • Understand cancellation and refund terms
